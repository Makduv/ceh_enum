How the simulator is designed

Questions intentionally mix short recognition items with longer scenario wording. During the exam, the domain/module is hidden so it cannot give away the answer. Explanations appear only after submission. Answer positions are balanced across A–D.

Use the five forms as separate attempts rather than reading the review first. The underlying curriculum overlaps by design, but question wording and coverage rotate across the forms.
